The High-Dimensional Challenge

  • Neuro-Fuzzy Systems suffer from the Curse of Dimensionality as rules grow exponentially with features.
  • During our early analysis, we observed that isolated pre-processing techniques (like PCA) destroy fuzzy interpretability.
  • Our Focus: We reproduced the ADAR dual-weighting mechanism to prove that models can actively build and prune their own architectures during gradient descent.

Key Insights & Limitations

  • Insights: We found that coupling the rule growth mechanics directly to validation patience effectively prevents underfitting in complex regions.
  • Thresholds: We opted for an attribute pruning threshold of $\theta_{attr} = 0.25$ because it securely isolated dead features while preserving variance.
  • Limitations: Hard-pruning an attribute irreversibly blocks its gradient. During our implementation, we observed this can sometimes lock the model out of delayed non-linear interactions. A soft-pruning decay might be a better future extension.
